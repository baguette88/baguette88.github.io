Common Questions

What is a Game Boy Color IPS mod?

An IPS mod replaces the original Game Boy Color's unlit reflective LCD with a modern IPS backlit display. The screen becomes dramatically brighter and sharper with adjustable brightness, making it usable in any lighting. The mod preserves the original shell and controls — it looks and feels authentic, it just has a screen that belongs in this decade.

What is a hall effect joystick?

The original N64 joystick uses plastic gears that grind against each other. Over time, they wear down and the stick gets loose and drifty. A hall effect module replaces that mechanical system with magnetic sensors. No physical contact means no wear, no drift, and consistent precision for the life of the controller. It drops into the OEM shell with no permanent modifications.

Is everything tested before shipping?

Yes. Game Boy Color IPS mods are tested across multiple games for screen quality, button response, speaker output, and battery life. N64 controllers are range-tested for joystick precision and checked for button and cable integrity. Consoles are tested with multiple cartridges.
